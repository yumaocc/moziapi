# MoziAPI Marketing Design

## Product Character

MoziAPI is a developer-facing API service. Marketing pages should feel direct,
technical, and trustworthy: show the offer, the product evidence, and the next
action without making users decode decorative copy.

## Visual Direction

- Keep the existing near-black canvas and mint-green brand accent.
- Use mint for the primary action and the single most important number in a
  viewport. Supporting information stays white or muted grey.
- Prefer thin borders, compact status rows, pricing receipts, and product-like
  data panels over illustration or atmospheric decoration.
- Use 6–10px radii for buttons and panels. Pills are reserved for status or
  campaign labels.
- Display headings use a medium weight, tight line height, and modest negative
  tracking. Code, prices, and operational labels use system monospace.

## First-Screen Hierarchy

The first viewport must answer, in order:

1. What is the current offer?
2. What product is this and why is it better?
3. What should the visitor do next?
4. What evidence reduces purchase or integration anxiety?

For the current campaign, “全模型 0.1 折” and “充值三倍到账” are the campaign
headline. Product proof should cover fast mainland access, resilient routing,
pay-as-you-go billing, transparent usage, no VPN, and no credit card.

## Layout and Responsive Rules

- Desktop hero: two columns, with value proposition on the left and a
  product-like campaign/billing panel on the right.
- Mobile hero: campaign strip, value proposition, actions, proof points, then
  the campaign panel. No horizontal scrolling.
- Primary and secondary actions must remain visible without competing fills.
- Touch targets are at least 44px tall; focus states must remain visible.
- Check headline wrapping, code/price overflow, panel order, and CTA width at
  1440px and 390px.

## Copy Rules

- Lead with concrete outcomes: fast, stable, direct, transparent, affordable.
- Avoid unverifiable uptime or latency numbers.
- Keep campaign claims labeled as activities and express recharge examples in
  USD for both the recharge amount and the account balance received.
- Checkout inputs, quick amounts, plan prices, and account balances use USD.
  CNY appears only on an explicitly labeled gateway-settlement line immediately
  before a WeChat Pay or other CNY payment.
- Prefer “无需翻墙”“无需信用卡”“按量计费”“消费透明” over generic claims such as
  “体验更好”.
